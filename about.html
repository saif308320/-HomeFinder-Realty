About us

Independent, local, and a little old-fashioned about service

We've been placing homes across Chelsea and the surrounding streets since 2007 — still under the same roof on King's Road.

2007 Founded on King's Road, Chelsea

Started by an agent who got tired of the industry's habits

James Whitfield opened HomeFinder Realty in 2007 after a decade at two of London's largest agencies, frustrated by inflated valuations and properties handed between departments. The idea was simple: keep the book small, keep one agent responsible for a home from valuation to completion, and never quote a price you don't believe.

Eighteen years on, the office is still on King's Road. The team has grown to eight, split across Chelsea, Fulham, Battersea and Kensington, but the original rule hasn't changed — every instruction gets a proper photographer, a floor plan, and a straight answer.
